Current website setup

As currently configured, noahdigital.io does not intentionally deploy advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or non-essential analytics cookies.

The website is primarily a static public website. Contact forms prepare an email in your default email application rather than submitting information to an analytics or marketing platform through the website.

Website hosting, content-delivery, security, or infrastructure providers may use essential technologies or process request information needed to deliver pages, prevent abuse, maintain availability, and protect the website. These technologies may differ depending on the final production hosting environment.

Third-party websites

When you follow a link to LinkedIn, DealsCanvas, CatchTheDeal.ai, or another external website, that site may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Those practices are controlled by the relevant third party and are governed by its policies.

Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking essential technologies may affect how some websites function.

Future analytics or advertising technology

If NOAH Digital adds non-essential analytics, advertising, or similar tracking technologies to this website, this policy should be updated and any consent mechanism required by applicable law should be implemented before those technologies are used.
