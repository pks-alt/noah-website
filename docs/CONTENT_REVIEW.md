# Content and visual review

This revision removes decorative arrow and emoji characters from the visible site, its HTML, and its active navigation script. Menu and Close are words. Links retain clear labels without icon suffixes.

Removed illustrations include simulated performance charts, live-status badges, orbit-style diagrams, and the two stylized commerce-property mockups. The approved NOAH logo is the only image used by the pages. Both commerce properties retain their names, descriptions, and outbound links.

The legacy homepage scripts were rewriting cleaned copy after page load. They and their script references have been removed. Page text is now authored directly in HTML and is not rewritten by client-side scripts.

Generic marketing sentences were replaced with concrete descriptions of customer acquisition, partner distribution, product/offer discovery, budgets, and campaign review. No client results, testimonials, certifications, or new numerical performance claims were invented.

Legal body copy is preserved, apart from punctuation normalization. Legal approval remains required, particularly the actual production privacy, form-delivery, and tracking practices.

A style cleanup cannot certify authorship or guarantee how every reader will perceive the copy. This review is about clarity, accuracy, and presentation.
