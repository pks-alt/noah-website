## Design principles
- Marketing-first, not SaaS-first.
- Editorial hierarchy and premium campaign-quality visuals.
- Fewer repetitive cards.
- Strong light/dark rhythm.
- Real screenshots for DealsCanvas, CatchTheDeal.ai, and Noah Intelligence when approved.
- Use only verified case-study results.
- Insights is intentionally not part of the homepage.
